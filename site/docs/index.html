What is Hoodpool?

The liquidity layer of Robinhood Chain. Swap any launched token today through canonical Uniswap. Next: one-click pools where LPs earn 1% of every swap, plus on-chain take-profit / stop-loss on positions that never leave your wallet.

Launch your token → deploy liquidity on Hoodpool → lock LP in Strongroom

Nobody holds your funds

  • Swaps route through canonical Uniswap — audited contracts that were already on-chain before we existed.
  • Non-custodial. Positions live in your wallet; the protocol holds nothing between transactions.
  • No owner, no admin keys, no upgrade hooks on Hoodpool contracts. What is deployed is what runs.
  • Factory-gated listings. A token lists only if its launch verifies on-chain.

Fees, simply

Swapping today: only Uniswap's standard pool fee — Hoodpool adds zero fee to swaps. When Hoodpool pools ship: every swap pays a 1% fee to the pool's LPs (that's you), and the protocol takes 0.1% of deposits, routed to the treasury. That's the whole model.
