What is Hoodpool?

Hoodpool is the liquidity layer for the launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Robinhood Chain is where tokens are born — Hoodpool is where their liquidity goes to work. Every token is verified on-chain against the LaunchFactory: if it wasn't launched on Robinhood Chain, it doesn't appear here.

Launch on Robinhood Chain → deploy liquidity on Hoodpool → lock LP in Strongroom

Nobody holds your funds

  • Positions are Uniswap NFTs that live in your wallet — Hoodpool never custodies them.
  • No owner, no admin keys, no upgrade hooks. What is deployed is what runs, forever.
  • One transaction per deposit: fee taken, liquidity minted, done. Between transactions the protocol holds nothing.
  • Every number links back to the chain. Nothing is served from a private database you have to trust.

Fees, simply

Every swap pays a 1% fee that goes to the pool's LPs — that's you. The protocol takes 0.1% of deposits, routed to the treasury. That's the whole model.

Take-profit and stop-loss orders execute on-chain via keepers — they can trigger while you sleep, and they never require handing over your position.
